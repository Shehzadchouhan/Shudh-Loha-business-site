The material

Why Sarbloh

Sarbloh is traditional iron cookware valued for its sturdy construction, strong heat retention, and long service life. With regular seasoning and careful drying, it is made for everyday cooking and can be maintained for years.

Getting started

First-time seasoning and care

  1. Wash the cookware with warm water and a small amount of mild dish soap, then dry it completely.
  2. Warm the cookware gently. Apply a very thin layer of cooking oil over the inside surface.
  3. Heat on low to medium until the oil dries and the surface darkens; let it cool fully. Repeat as needed.
  4. After cooking, wash promptly, dry fully, and wipe on a light coat of oil before storing in a dry place.
